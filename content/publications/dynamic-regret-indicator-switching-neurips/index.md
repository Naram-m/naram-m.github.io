abstract: We study dynamic regret in online convex optimization with an indicator switching cost, a fixed penalty incurred whenever two consecutive decisions differ. We propose a meta-learning framework that is minimax-optimal, up to logarithmic factors, for tracking piecewise-constant comparators, without prior knowledge of their number of switches or path length.
